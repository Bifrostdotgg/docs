The NamelessMC integration connects Discord accounts to profiles on a NamelessMC-powered forum. Once linked, Heimdall can verify forum membership and sync roles between the forum and Discord.

## Account linking

Members link their forum account to their Discord account using a verification command:

```
/forumlink
```

Running this command prompts the member to provide their NamelessMC forum username or email. The bot initiates a verification process through the NamelessMC API - the member confirms the link from their forum account settings page - and once confirmed, the accounts are paired.

After linking, Heimdall can automatically assign Discord roles based on forum group membership, and the customer portal can display the member's forum profile.

## Role synchronisation

When role sync is configured, Heimdall periodically checks each linked member's forum group membership and updates their Discord roles to match. This keeps your Discord access levels aligned with your forum structure automatically.

## Required permissions

| Action | Permission key |
|---|---|
| Linking a forum account | `namelessmc.use` (default allow) |
