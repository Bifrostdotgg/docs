The affiliate program pays out for servers that sign up to Heimdall through your referral. This page covers the Discord side of it: joining, getting your link, and checking how it is doing.

## Getting a link

`/affiliate join` enrols you and issues a referral code. `/affiliate view` shows your code, your link, and your current stats.

Your link is your code appended to the dashboard URL. Anyone who follows it and sets Heimdall up is attributed to you.

## Checking your stats

`/affiliate view` reports what has been attributed to your code so far. The reply is private to you.

## Required permissions

| Action | Permission key |
| --- | --- |
| Joining and viewing your stats | `affiliate.use` |

The command is global rather than per server, so it works anywhere the bot is present. It only ever shows your own figures.

<Note>
Payout rates, thresholds, and terms are handled outside Discord. This page documents the commands only.
</Note>
